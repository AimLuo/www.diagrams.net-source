1. Select _Arrange > Insert > Freehand_ to open the _Freehand_ dialog. 
<br />If you are using the [Sketch whiteboard-like editor theme](/blog/sketch-online-whiteboard.html), click on the _Freehand_ tool (pen icon).
2. Draw your shape with your mouse (hold the left-mouse button down to draw). 

When you let go of the left mouse button, or raise your finger or tablet pen on a mobile device, what you have drawn will be converted to a shape with a transparent background automatically. 

**To stop:** Click _Stop drawing_ to be able to select and style those shapes with the mouse - otherwise mouse clicks on the drawing canvas will continue to draw freehand shapes.

To create a freehand shape with a thicker line, ensure the _Brush_ checkbox is enabled and increase the slider width, then draw your freehand shape.
<br /><img src="/assets/img/blog/freehand-brush.gif" style="width=100%;max-width:500px;height:auto;" alt="Use the freehand drawing tool in draw.io to markup diagrams or draw shapes with your mouse or on a tablet">

While you can draw a freehand shape with the mouse, you may find it easier to [diagram freehand on a tablet](/doc/faq/mobile-diagram-app.html) with a pen.
